# Screen record — INGO

28 screens captured 21 September 2026 at 1080 × 2400 from the build described in
[`../BUILD-PROVENANCE.md`](../BUILD-PROVENANCE.md).

Each screen layout is independently protectable as an artistic work. All data
shown is demonstration data produced by the application offline — no real
person's information appears in any capture.

## Passenger interface

| # | Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Onboarding — certified drivers | First of three introduction panels: all drivers background-checked and verified |
| 02 | Onboarding — safety | SOS button, live trip tracking, 24/7 support |
| 03 | Onboarding — coverage | Kampala, Entebbe and Jinja |
| 04 | Passenger sign-in | Ugandan number entry with +256 prefix; separate driver entry point |
| 05 | Phone verification | Six-box one-time code entry with resend timer |
| 06 | Passenger home | Map, nearby-driver count, quick places, recent trips, SOS, booking action |
| 07 | Choose destination | Origin and destination fields over a list of popular Ugandan destinations |
| 08 | Ride types and fares | System estimate with EcoRide, ComfortRide and PremiumRide side by side, each with price, capacity and duration |
| 09 | Make your offer | **The negotiation screen.** Passenger names their own fare against the system estimate, with the minimum offer enforced and shortcut chips |
| 10 | Choose a driver | **The two-sided bidding screen.** Responding drivers with rating, trip count, vehicle, arrival time and each driver's counter-offer; auto-allocate alternative |
| 11 | Driver on the way | Live status header with ETA, elapsed time, distance, agreed fare, chat, call and SOS |
| 12 | Trip in progress | The same live panel during the journey |
| 13 | Trip complete and payment | Arrival confirmation with itemised fare — base, distance, time — total, and payment method recorded |
| 14 | Rate your ride | Star rating with positive-attribute tags |
| 15 | Trip history | Past trips with route, date and fare |
| 16 | Safety centre | Emergency hotline, SOS alert, auto-share and audio-recording settings, emergency contacts |
| 17 | Account | Profile, lifetime stats, and entries to the marketplace, saved places, payment methods, history and contacts |
| 18 | Services marketplace | Six categories — mechanics, spare parts, car wash, tyres, accessories, drivers |
| 19 | Payment methods | Cash and mobile-money options |
| 20 | Saved places | Home, work and custom locations |
| 21 | Emergency contacts | Contacts notified on an SOS |

## Driver interface

| # | Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | Driver landing | Recruitment screen — earnings share, safety, flexible hours, rating growth |
| 23 | Driver benefits | Indicative weekly earnings, with apply and sign-in actions |
| 24 | Driver sign-in | Registered-number entry with code verification |
| 25 | Driver dashboard | Online/offline switch, map, and the day's trips, earnings and hours |
| 26 | Driver online | The dashboard in its online state, available for requests |
| 27 | Driver earnings and float | **The settlement screen.** Float balance with top-up and withdraw, period earnings, platform commission, trips, hours, average fare and acceptance rate |
| 28 | Driver profile | Driver details and settings, including client preference and the marketplace entry |

---

*Screens 09, 10 and 27 carry the features that most distinguish this work: the
passenger-named fare, the driver counter-offer market, and the driver float from
which commission settles.*
